Simple IoT is developed in the open, and questions from people building real
systems shape where it goes next. Whichever of these you prefer, we would like
to hear from you.

## Ask a question or start a discussion

The [Simple IoT community forum](https://community.tmpdir.org/c/simple-iot/5) is
the best place for questions, ideas, and longer discussions. Threads stay
searchable, so an answer today helps the next person too.

## Report a bug or request a feature

Open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/simpleiot/simpleiot/issues). Issues
labeled "help wanted" and "good first issue" are a good place to start if you
would like to contribute.

## Contribute

Pull requests are welcome. The
[development guide](https://docs.simpleiot.org/docs/ref/development.html) covers
the architecture, tooling, and how to get a development environment running.
Simple IoT is licensed under Apache 2.0.

## Follow along

- [Blog](/blog/) and [Atom feed](/atom.xml) for project news
- [Email newsletter](https://tmpdir.ck.page/196d1fb480) for periodic updates
- [YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@simple-iot) for demos and
  walkthroughs, also collected on the [videos page](/videos/)
- [GitHub releases](https://github.com/simpleiot/simpleiot/releases) for the
  changelog

## Tell us what you built

If Simple IoT is running in something you have shipped, or you are evaluating it
and something is missing, let us know. Hearing from users is genuinely helpful
in deciding what to work on next.
